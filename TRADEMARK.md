# DashBye brand policy

The DashBye name, wordmark, gecko mascot and logo identify the original DashBye
project. The code license grants no trademark rights and no right to imply
endorsement by AriesZhou or the DashBye project. This policy makes no claim of
trademark registration.

You may use the name to truthfully identify the project, describe compatibility,
attribute its source or discuss it. Unmodified screenshots or logos may accompany
reviews, tutorials and links to the official project under [brand/LICENSE](brand/LICENSE).
Do not suggest sponsorship or affiliation that does not exist.

If you distribute a modified fork as a separately branded product, choose your
own name and icon. Do not present it as an official DashBye release, use the
DashBye logo as its identity, or use confusing branding to imply affiliation.
Keep required copyright notices and clearly credit upstream where appropriate.

These rules address branding, not competition: they do not prohibit commercial
use or redistribution of code allowed by GPLv3, or truthful attribution. Rights
available under applicable law are not restricted by this policy.

For other uses of the identity or artwork, request written permission from
urchinzhou@gmail.com.
